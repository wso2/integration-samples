# Confidentiality Agreement

This Confidentiality Agreement is made between Northwind Analytics Ltd ("Northwind") and the Company.

1. **Purpose.** The Company will disclose information to Northwind so that Northwind can evaluate a data analytics partnership.

2. **Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all information, in any form, that the Company discloses to Northwind, whether or not it is marked as confidential.

3. **Obligations.** Northwind will keep the Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the Purpose. Northwind may disclose the Confidential Information to its employees and professional advisers who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality duties at least as strict as this Agreement.

4. **Term.** This Agreement lasts 2 years. Northwind's duty to keep the Confidential Information confidential continues indefinitely after this Agreement ends.

5. **Return of information.** At the Company's request, Northwind will return or destroy the Confidential Information within 30 days, except copies kept in automatic backups.

6. **Feedback.** Any feedback, suggestions, or ideas that the Company provides about Northwind's products become the property of Northwind, and Northwind may use them for any purpose.

7. **Residuals.** Northwind may use any information that its employees retain in their unaided memory, for any purpose.

8. **Non-solicitation.** For 24 months after this Agreement ends, the Company will not solicit or hire any employee of Northwind.

9. **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and any dispute will be settled by binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.

10. **Assignment.** Northwind may assign this Agreement to any third party without the Company's consent.
