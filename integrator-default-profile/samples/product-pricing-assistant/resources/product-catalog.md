# Meeting Software Product Catalog

## Starter Plan

The Starter plan is designed for small teams getting started with video meetings.

- HD video meetings for up to 10 participants
- 40-minute meeting time limit
- Screen sharing
- Basic chat during meetings

The Starter plan does not include meeting recording.

## Pro Plan

The Pro plan is designed for growing teams that need longer, larger meetings.

- HD video meetings for up to 100 participants
- Unlimited meeting duration
- Screen sharing
- Breakout rooms
- Meeting transcripts (text only)

The Pro plan does not include meeting recording.

## Business Plan

The Business plan is designed for organizations that need to capture and revisit meetings.

- HD video meetings for up to 300 participants
- Unlimited meeting duration
- Screen sharing
- Breakout rooms
- **Cloud meeting recording**, with recordings saved automatically and shareable via link
- Meeting transcripts with speaker labels
- Admin usage dashboard

The Business plan is the lowest-tier plan that includes meeting recording.

## Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan is designed for large organizations with advanced security and support needs.

- HD video meetings for up to 1000 participants
- Unlimited meeting duration
- Screen sharing
- Breakout rooms
- Cloud meeting recording, with recordings saved automatically and shareable via link
- Meeting transcripts with speaker labels and AI summaries
- Single sign-on (SSO) and advanced security controls
- Dedicated customer support
